dashboard.example.com

Dashboard on a phone

The mock on the left works: tap rows, the ⋯ buttons, the thread rows, the filter chips and the + in the bottom bar. Sample data is made up. Everything here applies below 640px only. Desktop and the current 640–1080px stacked layout stay as they are.

What's broken today (from the screenshots)

  • The nav rail takes about 90 of 390px, so every panel is squeezed into the rest.
  • Task titles cut off after 4–5 characters. The date column wraps to three lines ("7 / Oct / 202").
  • The calendar strip shows up as an empty white card.
  • Thread rows: the step text gets 1 character ("p"), and the pills and +1 push past the edge.
  • Scratchpad: the placeholder, RECENT, the notes and the Capture button all draw on top of each other.
  • Each panel gets a 60vh minimum height, so you scroll through four tall boxes to find anything.
M1

Bottom bar instead of the nav rail

Home · News · + · Dev · Me. Settings, the avatar and sign-out move into a "Me" sheet. The page gets its full width back. The + opens quick capture from any view.

Alternative: a hamburger menu in the header. It saves the bottom space but hides views behind an extra tap.

M2

One section at a time, behind tabs

A sticky tab row: My Tasks · Follow-ups · Threads · Scratch, each with its count. You see one list at full height and switch with a tap. The header (date, agenda, tabs) stays put while the list scrolls.

Alternative: Stacked. All four sections on one scrolling page, each collapsible, with no fixed heights. Use the "Stacked" toggle above the phone to compare.

M3

Calendar becomes a swipeable agenda row

The hour axis can't fit 390px. Instead: one row of event cards, past ones dimmed, a red "now" marker, and the next event highlighted. Day arrows stay in the header.

Alternative: keep the axis but show only ±3 hours around now. It's closer to desktop but cramped.

M4

Task rows: title first, everything else behind ⋯

A 44px checkbox, then the title across the full width (up to 2 lines), then the thread chip on a line below it. The drag handle and the date icon go away. ⋯ opens a sheet with Today / Tomorrow / Next week / Pick date, Move to list, Edit, Delete, and Open thread when the task has one. These are the desktop row actions, nothing new. Completing a task shows the usual Undo toast.

Locked: no drag on mobile. Reordering and grouping stay desktop-only. Existing groups still render, read-only.

M5

Threads: two-line rows, filters scroll sideways

Line 1: title, age, ⋯. Line 2: the next step in full, with the whose-move pill and "+1 step" under it. The filter chips sit in one row that scrolls sideways. "+ thread" moves to the section header. The list/graph toggle is hidden because only the list view works at this width.

M6

Scratchpad: plain vertical flow, plus quick capture

The editor grows with its text, a full-width Capture button sits below it, and Recent comes last. Nothing overlaps. The bottom-bar + opens the same editor as a sheet, so you can capture something without leaving the tab you're on.

M7

Dev view gets the same pass

Dev already has tabs. On a phone its header stacks, the lane tabs scroll sideways, and draft cards and their edit forms take the full width. News only has to fit without sideways scrolling; a proper News pass comes later.
