Habits: small daily things, tracked lightly

The frame below works. Tick habits, tap a habit's name for its last four weeks, tap a past dot this week to backfill, use ⋯ to edit or drop, and press Next day to see the badge clear at midnight. The sample habits come from your examples. The date is fixed at Thu 8 Oct so the week has some history to show.

H1

Its own rail entry, behind a habits flag

Habits sits under Dev in the rail. It uses the same per-user flag as News and Dev: the superuser always has it, and turns it on for others in Settings › People. Adding the flag is one entry in the feature registry plus a habits/gating.py that copies dev/gating.py. The flag gates the rail entry, the badge fetch and every /habits/* endpoint.

H2

A habit is a name and a weekly target

The target is Daily or N times a week (3–6). You don't pick weekdays, a time of day or a reminder. Weeks run Monday to Sunday.

Alternative: pick specific weekdays (e.g. Mon/Wed/Fri). That's more precise, but "4–5 days a week" is about flexibility, so a count fits better.

H3

The nudge is a number on the rail

The Habits icon shows how many habits are still open today: not ticked yet, and still short of this week's target. A 3×/week habit you've already done 3 times this week drops out of the count, so the number only asks for what you still need. When it reaches 0 the badge disappears. At midnight it starts again from the new day's list. Yesterday's misses don't carry over.

Alternative: count every habit you haven't ticked today, whatever the weekly target. It's simpler, but a 4×/week habit would nag you seven days a week.

H4

Tracking stays small

  • Each row: this week as seven dots (filled = done, dashed = missed, ringed = today), the week's count against the target, and the streak.
  • Streak: daily habits count days in a row. N×/week habits count weeks in a row where the target was hit. Not having ticked today, or not having hit the target yet this week, doesn't break a streak.
  • Tap the name: a 4-week grid, the hit rate, and the start date.

Misses show as a muted dashed dot, not red. No charts beyond four weeks.

H5

Cut losses: one prompt, then Drop

Once a habit is two weeks old, if it hit less than half its target over the last 14 days, a single line appears under it: Keep, Ease the target, or Drop. Keep hides the prompt for a week. Drop takes the habit out of today's list and the badge, and keeps its history in a collapsed "Dropped" list where you can revive it. "Piano, 5 minutes" in the mock shows this.

Alternative: no prompt. You drop habits yourself from ⋯ whenever you choose.

H6

"Today" is your browser's local date

The client sends its local date, and a tick is stored as a plain date (no time). The midnight reset happens in your timezone, wherever you are. You can backfill earlier days of the current week by tapping their dots (for days you did it but forgot to tick). You can't tick future days. Weeks before this one are read-only.

H7

Data: two tables, no Google, no LLM

  • habit: id, user_id, title, target_per_week (1–7), created_on, dropped_on, hint_snoozed_until.
  • habit_check: habit_id, day. Unique per (habit, day). Untick deletes the row.
  • GET /habits?today= returns the active and dropped habits with the last 5 weeks of ticks. Streaks, counts and the badge number are worked out from that payload on the client, so the rail and the view can't disagree.
  • Writes: create, edit, drop/revive, tick and untick. Alembic migration, SQLite and Postgres.
H8

Phone: Habits joins the bottom bar

With the flag on, the bar becomes Home · News · + · Dev · Habits · Me, which is six slots. The badge sits on the Habits icon. Switch the frame to Phone to see it. The week dots move under the title.

Alternative: keep five slots and put Habits inside the Me sheet. That hides the badge, which is the whole nudge.

Questions for you

Q1

Badge on the Habits icon, not Dev?

You said "on the dev rail I see a 2". I read that as "on the rail", with the number on Habits' own icon. If you want the count to show on Home too (e.g. in the header), say so.

Q2

Badge rule: H3 (the week's need) or every unticked habit?

H3 recommends dropping a habit out of the count once its weekly target is met.

Q3

Also show the count in the browser tab title?

"(2) Dashboard" would show even when the dashboard is a background tab. It stays inside the dashboard and costs one line. It's on in the mock. Off is fine too.

Q4

Should a tick carry a note?

For "write a paragraph", you could write the paragraph right on the tick. I'd leave it out of v0: the habit is the doing, and the writing lives wherever you write. If you want it, the Scratchpad capture is the better home.

Q5

The "Keep / Ease / Drop" prompt (H5): in or out?

The threshold is under 50% of target over 14 days, and only after a habit is two weeks old. Tune the numbers, or skip the prompt for v0.

Q6

How far back can you backfill?

H6 allows the current week only. "Yesterday only" is stricter. "Any day" is looser, and then the streak numbers get easier to fudge.

Out of v0: reminders or notifications of any kind, specific weekdays, times of day, notes on ticks, reordering (habits stay in creation order), sharing habits between users, and history views beyond four weeks.
